# Claims & Land Protection (GriefPrevention)

Hoobs Live uses the **GriefPrevention** protection system to keep your builds, chests, and resources 100% safe from griefing, theft, and explosions.

---

## ⛏️ How to Claim Land

1. **Get Claim Tools**:
   - In stream chat or in-game, type:
     `
     !shovel
     `
     You will receive a **Golden Shovel** (for claiming) and a **Stick** (for checking claim boundaries and owners).
2. **Mark Your Corners**:
   - Right-click one corner block of your desired area with the golden shovel.
   - Walk diagonally to the opposite corner and right-click another block.
   - Gold and glowstone marker blocks will appear momentarily to show your active claim borders.
3. **Expand a Claim**:
   - Hold your golden shovel, right-click any existing corner marker, and right-click further out to expand the claim boundaries.

---

## 📋 Claim Commands

| Command | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| /claim | Automatically creates a claim around your current location (if you have enough blocks). |
| /trust <player> | Grants full building, container, and interaction access to a trusted friend. |
| /containertrust <player> | Allows a player to open chests, furnaces, hoppers, and animal pens only. |
| /accesstrust <player> | Allows a player to use doors, buttons, levers, and beds only. |
| /permissiontrust <player> | Allows another player to manage /trust permissions for your claim. |
| /untrust <player> | Revokes all permissions for a player. |
| /trustlist | Displays all players currently trusted in the active claim. |
| /claimslist | Lists all your active claims and your available claim block total. |
| /abandonclaim | Deletes the claim you are currently standing inside, refunding all claim blocks. |
| /abandonallclaims | Deletes all claims you own across the world. |
| /subdivideclaims | Switches shovel to sub-division mode (allows renting or assigning sub-plots). |

## 🔔 Discord Claim Expiration Notifications

To prevent active player builds from being lost due to prolonged inactivity:
- If your land claims are approaching expiration due to inactivity, the server will **automatically send a direct message (DM) to your linked Discord account** alerting you to log in and preserve your claims.
- Make sure your Discord is linked by typing !discord in chat or running /discord link on the server!

---

## 💡 Claim Tips

- **Earning Blocks**: You earn claim blocks passively for every hour you play on the server.
- **Buying Blocks**: Additional claim blocks can also be purchased with Hoob Loot via the stream !shop.
- **Inspecting Land**: Right-click the ground with a plain **Stick** at any time to see if an area is claimed and who owns it.
