# Viewer Ranks & Watch-Hour Milestones

Hoobs Live rewards long-time stream viewers with custom **Viewer Ranks**, in-game scoreboard colors, glowing NPC cosmetics, Discord roles, and unlocked chat command permissions.

---

## 🏆 Viewership Rank Tiers

Every minute you watch the livestream is tracked automatically. As you accumulate watch hours, your rank upgrades:

| Rank | Required Watch Time | Name Color / Badge | Unlocked Permissions & Perks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LINKED** | 0+ Hours | ⚪ White | Base viewer rank upon linking account with !app. |
| **DAY** | **24+ Hours** (1 Day) | 🩵 Aqua (#55FFFF) | Unlocks the ability to rob viewers with !rob. |
| **REGULAR** | **168+ Hours** (7 Days) | 🔷 Dark Aqua (#2ABFBF) | Unlocks placing player bounties with !bounty. |
| **MONTH** | **720+ Hours** (30 Days) | 🔴 Red (#FF5555) | Veteran status, advanced NPC armor tiers, jail leniency. |
| **SIX MONTH** | **4,380+ Hours** (6 Months) | 🟥 Dark Red (#AA0000) | Elite status, high-tier NPC cosmetic loadouts. |
| **YEAR** | **8,760+ Hours** (1 Year) | 🟪 Dark Purple (#AA00AA) | Legend status, maximum NPC prestige rewards. |

## ⭐ Membership / Sponsor Rank

YouTube Channel Members receive the **MEMBER** tier (#00AA00 Dark Green) and exclusive stream powers:

- !say & !sing — Free Text-to-Speech voice messages on stream.
- !sheep [player] [color] — Drop 2X sheep with custom color picker.
- !bell — 10X the bell rings.
- !jeb / !jeb_ — Exclusive rainbow jeb_ sheep.
- !soundboard — Exclusive soundboard audio effects.
- !npc — Glowing custom NPC aura.
- Private Members-Only chat channel in Discord.

---

## 🛡️ Moderator & Staff Ranks

- **MODS** — YouTube Chat and In-Game Moderators display a protective mod shield on their player head, custom stream camera priority, and access to !ban, !jail, and !pardon.
- **HOOBS** — Server Owner / Host (#FFAA00 Gold).

---

## 📊 How to Check Your Rank & Hours

1. **In YouTube Stream Chat**:
   Type:
   `
   !loot
   `
   or !bal. BlexBot will reply with your current watch hours, rank badge, loot total, and NPC combat record.
2. **In the Companion App**:
   Visit [https://app.hoobs.live](https://app.hoobs.live) to view your rank card, trophy medals, and hours progress bar.
