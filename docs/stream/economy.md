# Stream Economy, Bonuses & Interactive Shop

Hoobs Live features a full virtual economy where stream viewers earn **Hoob Loot** to spend on interactive server events, custom items, and player rewards.

---

## 💰 How to Earn Hoob Loot

| Command | Reward | Frequency / Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| !free | Dynamic Loot | Collect free loot every **1 minute**. Amount scales based on recent activity. |
| !daily | 1 to 1,000,000 Loot | Claim once every **24 hours**. |
| !weekly | 1 to 5,000,000 Loot | Claim once every **7 days**. |
| !monthly | 1 to 10,000,000 Loot | Claim once every **30 days**. |
| !bacon | Up to 110,000 Loot | Every 5 minutes, the bacon heats up. Grab it during the 30s crispy window! |
| !code <code> | 1 to 5,000,000 Loot | Redeem 2FA live stream codes or secret codes hidden in YouTube Shorts. |
| !raffle <number> | Large Loot Pool | When an automated raffle starts, pick the winning lucky number! |
| !loot | Balance Check | Check your current loot, watch hours, NPC rank, and active perks. |

## ⚔️ Robberies & Police Protection

Viewers can attempt to rob loot from other viewers in the stream:

- **Rob a Viewer**:
  `
  !rob <viewer>
  `
  or
  `
  !rob #1
  `
  *(Robs the #1 richest player on the leaderboard)*
- **Success Rate**: 30% chance of a successful robbery.
- **Cooldown**: 10-minute cooldown on failed robberies; 30-minute cooldown on successful robberies.
- **Fail Audio**: When a robbery fails, a custom audio clip plays on stream!
- **Police Protection (!police)**:
  - Hire police for **1,000,000 Hoob Loot** to receive **12 hours of 100% robbery immunity**.

## 🛒 Interactive Shop & In-Game Actions

The live stream shop rotates its inventory every 10 minutes. Items purchased in chat trigger real-time actions and spawns in the Minecraft world:

### Core Shop Features
- !shop — View the 4 currently rotating items and their prices.
- !refresh — Manually refresh the shop rotation (costs 100,000 Loot, 1 min cooldown).
- **🎁 Loyalty Perk**: Every **10th purchase of the same item** is **100% FREE**!
- **🏷️ 50% OFF Coupons**: Unlocked from Ms Bacon's Code Wheel (!code), coupons automatically apply half-price to your next purchase.

### Interactive Commands
- !diamond <player> — Send a real Minecraft diamond to an in-game player (5 min cooldown).
- !sheep <player> [color] — Drop a colored sheep on a player (Members get 2X sheep and custom color selection).
- !shovel — Receive a golden land claim shovel and inspection stick in-game.
- !letter <player> <message> — Send an in-game written book directly to a player's inventory.
- !surprise — Deliver a random surprise gift item to a random online player.
- !xp <player> — Send XP orbs to a player (level scales with weekly usage, up to Level 10).
- !bounty <player> <amount> — Place a loot bounty on a player (minimum 1,000,000 Loot). Defeating the player in PvP awards the bounty!
- !wanted — View all currently open player bounties.
- !melt <player> — Melt all snow within 10 blocks of a player (costs 1M Loot).
- !pow <player> — Zap all sheep within 10 blocks of a player (costs 1M Loot).
- !paint <x> <y> <color> — Place a colored concrete block on the community canvas at spawn.
